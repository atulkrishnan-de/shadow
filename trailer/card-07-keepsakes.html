He walked in that morning
with his pockets full.

The field has been stripping them off him,
one cycle at a time.

  • A watch, stopped at 09:41
  • A photograph, face down
  • A house key, worn smooth
  • A letter he never posted
  • A drawing his daughter did at the kitchen table

Picking one up puts a piece of him back.
