# The Last Shift — Trailer Shoot Guide

Screen-record these gameplay clips **between** the flashcards.
Save files into `trailer/clips/` using the filenames below, then set `src` in `trailer/index.html` → `SEQUENCE`.

**Recording tips**
- Hide / ignore debug UI if any is visible
- Prefer continuous takes (no camera cuts mid-clip — matches game language)
- Capture with game audio on; trim dead air later
- Target lengths are trim targets, not hard caps — record a little long

## Clip A — after Card 01 · Cold Open

**Where:** Room I · INTAKE  
**Storyboard:** `01.1` Establish  
**Length:** ~4–6s  

**What to record**
1. Enter / load Room I so the entry camera pull-back plays
2. Hold while room card is on screen: `ROOM I — INTAKE` / *you have been here before*
3. Player idle under the single working lamp; corridor past the door stays dark

**Must see:** wide → ease-in framing, one lamp, empty dark room ahead  
**Must hear:** room tone only (no music)

## Clip B — after Card 02 · Sector C

**Where:** Hub (between rooms)  
**Storyboard:** hub walk — not a numbered panel; establish the plant  
**Length:** ~5–8s  

**What to record**
1. Spawn / walk the hub corridors and stairs
2. Pass locked room doors (especially ones that need keys)
3. Show scale: empty walkways, industrial height, no people

**Must see:** stairs + at least one locked door + empty plant volume  
**Avoid:** entering a room mid-clip (keep this as “outside the anomaly”)

## Clip C — after Card 03 · The Programme

**Where:** Room I · INTAKE  
**Storyboard:** `01.2` The rule, stated by the room  
**Length:** ~6–8s  

**What to record**
1. Settled gameplay framing — player left, door readable right
2. Step on plate → door lifts
3. Step off → door drops
4. Do it once more so the rule is unmistakable
5. Keep the timer visible in frame

**Must see:** plate + door in same shot; timer counting  
**Must hear:** plate clunk, door servo up/down

## Clip E — after Card 05 · The Loop

**Where:** Room I · INTAKE  
**Storyboard:** `01.4` The clock runs out  
**Length:** ~3–5s (includes black)  

**What to record**
1. Stand on the plate with time running out (or wait until <5s)
2. Capture shake + red vignette pulse
3. Timer hits `00:00` → white flash → hard cut to black (~0.85s)

**Must see:** timer → `00:00`, flash, black  
**Setup tip:** die while holding the plate so Clip F’s Shadow is already “useful”

## Clip F — after Card 06 · Shadows

**Storyboard:** `01.5` + `01.6` First Shadow · Walking past yourself

## Clip I — after Card 09 · The Sacrifice

**Where:** Room III · PRESS ROOM  
**Storyboard:** `03.1` → `03.4`  
**Length:** ~12–18s (can trim into two takes if needed)  

**What to record (prefer one continuous take)**
1. Entry: press completes a full slam under the room card
2. Brief look into the pit — pack already in water (`Your pack is already down there`)
3. Climb down onto the plate under the hammer and **wait**
4. Impact / death
5. On next attempt: Shadow takes the plate; you cross above; door opens; slam behind you

**Must see:** voluntary climb down + crossing while Shadow holds  
**Must hear:** press cycle → silence → impact → door / steps / slam

## Suggested recording order (by room)

Shoot in gameplay order so you don’t unlock backwards:

1. **Hub** — Clip B  
2. **Room I** — Clips A, C, D, E, F, G (one session; F needs a prior death)  
3. **Room II** — Clip H  
4. **Room III** — Clip I  
5. **Room IV** — Clips J, K, L  
6. **Ending** — Clips M, N

## Wire clips into the trailer

In `trailer/index.html`, find the matching clip in `SEQUENCE` and set `src`, e.g.:

```js
{ type: 'clip', id: 'F', src: 'clips/f-first-shadow.mp4', label: 'First Shadow',
  hint: '…', duration: 5000, skipIfEmpty: true },
```

Empty `src` clips stay skipped until you add the file.
